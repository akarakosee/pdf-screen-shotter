### Tips for Creating the Perfect PDF
- **Ordering is Key:** When you drop your images into the tool above, you can simply click and drag the thumbnail icons to rearrange the order of the pages before you generate the PDF.
- **Orientation:** If you are uploading photos taken on your phone, they might be in a mix of portrait and landscape orientations. The tool automatically centers them on the PDF page so the final document looks professional.
- **Privacy:** Identity documents like Driver's Licenses and Passports are the most commonly converted images. We guarantee that your images are never sent to a server. The PDF generation happens directly in your browser using local Javascript.
