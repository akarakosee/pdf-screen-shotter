When you are applying for a mortgage, preparing your taxes, or organizing financial records, you often need to submit your bank statements as a single, contiguous PDF document.

The biggest concern? **Privacy.** Bank statements contain highly sensitive information including your account numbers, transaction history, and home address. You should **never** upload these documents to a random server on the internet to merge them.

That's why we built this completely secure, offline-first tool.

## Merge Your Statements Right Here

You don't need to download any software. Use our secure merger right inside your browser. **Your files never leave your device.** The merging process happens directly using your device's memory, guaranteeing 100% privacy.

### How to use it:
1. **Upload your files:** Drag and drop all your monthly bank statement PDFs into the box above.
2. **Reorder them:** If they are out of chronological order, simply drag the files to rearrange them.
3. **Merge:** Click the merge button. Your browser will combine them instantly.
4. **Download:** Save the combined file directly to your computer.

### Why is this safe?
Traditional online PDF tools upload your file to their cloud servers, process it, and send it back to you. This means a third-party server temporarily (or permanently) stores your financial records.

Our tool runs entirely using WebAssembly and Web Workers *inside your browser*. Once the page loads, you can disconnect from the internet and the tool will still work flawlessly. Your bank statements remain strictly on your local machine.
