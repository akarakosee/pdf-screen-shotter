### Why Do PDFs Get So Big?
PDFs are usually bloated for three reasons:
1. **Unoptimized Images:** Scanners often save pages as massive, uncompressed TIFF or PNG images wrapped inside a PDF.
2. **Embedded Fonts:** Sometimes, software embeds entire font families (even if you only used 3 letters of that font) into the document.
3. **Hidden Metadata & Garbage:** Older PDF editors leave a history of every change you ever made in the code of the file.
